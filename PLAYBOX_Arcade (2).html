A SMALL PLACE TO PLAY

WELCOME TO PLAYBOX

PLAYBOX is a free little internet arcade: a collection of games, brain teasers, sound toys and interactive experiments made for curiosity, quick breaks and the joy of pressing buttons just to see what happens.

Pick up and play

No account or download required. Choose a game and jump straight in.

Something for every mood

Quick brain games, playful sound experiments, visual surprises and hands-on physics toys.

Play at your own pace

No streaks, no pressure and no need to compete. Stay for a minute or a while.

Why it exists

PLAYBOX is built around a simple idea: the web can still be a place for small, delightful things. The aim is to keep the games easy to access, fun to explore and free to play.

It is an evolving project. Some experiments may be simpler than others, and the collection will keep getting polished as it grows.

Found a broken game or have an idea? Contact details are coming soon.

THE BORING BUT IMPORTANT BIT

PRIVACY NOTES

This version of PLAYBOX does not include an account system, advertising tracker or analytics script. The games run in your browser.

Your browser's local storage is used for one small thing on your own device: your chosen colours. Nothing is sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data removes it.

The page loads one pixel font (Pixelify Sans) from Google Fonts, so your browser may contact Google's font servers. If that fails, a fallback monospace font is used. No other files are loaded from other sites.

Some browser features, such as audio, fullscreen and local storage, depend on your device and browser settings. No payment details are collected by PLAYBOX itself; if you choose to donate, the payment provider's own privacy terms apply.

This is a plain-language summary of the current page, not legal advice. Review it again whenever you add fonts, analytics, advertising, accounts, external embeds or other services.
